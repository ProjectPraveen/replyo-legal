Sign Up for SMS Updates

Replyo provides automated SMS communication services on behalf of local small businesses including plumbers, HVAC technicians, electricians, and other service providers.

How to Opt In

To receive messages from a business powered by Replyo, you can opt in one of two ways:

Option 1 — Text to opt in:
Text START to the business phone number you are contacting. By texting START you consent to receive automated SMS messages from that business regarding your service inquiry, appointments, and updates.

Option 2 — Web form opt in:
When submitting a service request or inquiry form for a Replyo-powered business, check the box that states: "I agree to receive text messages from this business regarding my inquiry. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time."

Option 3 — Inbound text:
When you text a business phone number powered by Replyo directly to ask about services or request an appointment, you consent to receive automated SMS replies related to your inquiry.

What Messages You Will Receive

Messages may include:

- Responses to your service inquiries

- Appointment confirmations and reminders

- Follow-up messages related to your request

- Business information such as hours and services

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on your inquiry. Typically 1 to 5 messages per conversation.

Message and Data Rates

Message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile carrier plan.

How to Opt Out

Reply STOP to any message at any time to unsubscribe. You will receive one confirmation message and no further messages will be sent.
